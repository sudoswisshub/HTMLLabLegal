    We believe in absolute data ownership. Discover how we protect your source code, browsing logs, and developer workspace.

    1. Information We Collect

    HTMLLab is engineered from the ground up to respect developer privacy. We do NOT collect, aggregate, or harvest your personal identifiers, IP addresses, native system configurations, or user-specific activities.

    The Application works strictly locally, with the following parameters:

    • Personal Data: We do not ask for, store, or transmit name, phone numbers, or account registrations. There are no registration forms within the Application.
    • Telemetry & Diagnostics: No continuous analytical trackers (like Google Analytics or Firebase) are integrated. Standard, non-identifying crash logs may be collected through the Apple App Store console if you have explicitly opted into sharing crash reports with app developers.

    2. Local Workspace & Sandboxing

    Any code drafts, CSS style configurations, custom JavaScript functions, bookmarks, diagnostics, and browsing history entries you input or load inside the Application reside strictly within your device's isolated application storage container.

    This data is managed in the following ways:

    • UserDefaults: Stores non-sensitive user interface preference defaults (such as font size, selected color themes, default search providers, and auto-run configurations).
    • WebKit Web Database Storage: Session cookies, isolated caches, and custom sandboxed DOM tree states are held in the standard, local WebKit cache directories and remain completely under your control.
    • Zero Cloud Telemetry: Code scripts are compiled and parsed locally on your device's CPU/GPU and are never uploaded to remote cloud compilation systems.

    3. Third-Party Websites & Services

    The Application includes an in-app browser client designed to allow you to audit active, live web links. When you query third-party web addresses in the URL input bar or follow active links:

    • Your device initiates a direct, secure handshake with the corresponding website's servers, subject to that specific platform's privacy policy.
    • HTMLLab exerts no control over third-party scripts, cookie injections, or security measures present on external sites. We encourage you to run in-app audits (such as the visual audits tab) to monitor and verify third-party security compliance.

    4. Data Security

    Since your sandbox developer drafts and browsing records are saved locally, the primary security of your data relies on your device's operating system security (macOS, iOS, or iPadOS). We leverage standard Apple sandboxing APIs to prevent other, non-authorized applications on your device from inspecting or reading your HTMLLab workspace directory structures.

    We advise developers to keep their systems updated and utilize standard device encryption mechanisms (such as FileVault or iOS Data Protection) to keep local code drafts safe.

    5. Your Privacy Rights & Control

    Because we do not store, manage, or hold your data on any cloud servers, we are unable to search for, retrieve, or delete your data on demand. You hold 100% direct control over your records:

    • Wiping Session Records: You can completely clear cookies, browsing history, saved bookmarks, and custom draft codes at any time by navigating to the Storage & Session Actions pane inside the Application's settings view.
    • App Deletion: Removing the HTMLLab application from your Apple device immediately and permanently deletes all associated sandboxed developer drafts and caches stored in the local application container.

    6. Children's Privacy

    Our developer utility does not address individuals under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not intentionally process or retain data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has transmitted any feedback to us via support channels, please contact us so we can immediately purge those correspondence records.
